Bulk DM campaign

Make each message unique with {Hi|Hello|Hey} options and {name} for the contact's name — every recipient gets a different version.
Example: {Hi|Hello} {name}, our weekend promo is live! {Don't miss out|Grab it now}.

A message carries text plus one attachment at most (image, voice note, or PDF).

A/B test (optional)

Write 2–3 message variants. Recipients are split evenly and randomly; a reply counts when someone messages you back within 72 hours of their send. The winner needs at least 10 sends per variant.

Product promo helper

Fill in a product and drop the generated message into the composer above.

Follow-up (optional)

After the campaign finishes, send a second message — only to people who never replied. Leave blank to skip.
